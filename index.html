EarFour

You can play the chords. You just can't get them into the track without typing chord names or drawing MIDI. Play 4 bars, one chord per bar, on a guitar or piano into your mic, a MIDI keyboard, or the keys below. EarFour hears each bar, writes the chord down, and seats a real sampled band (piano, finger bass, strings, drums) on exactly those chords at your BPM. Every loop pass is a new take. Nothing is uploaded; no AI sounds.

iOS: the silent switch mutes Web Audio.

How it works (3 steps)
  1. Set your tempo. Pick a groove or type a BPM. Headphones on.
  2. Press ● Record and, after one count-in bar, play one chord per bar for 4 bars, on anything.
  3. Press B. The band plays the chords EarFour heard. Fix any chord by typing it, then export.
